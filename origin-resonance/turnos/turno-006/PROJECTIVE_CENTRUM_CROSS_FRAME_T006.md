# T006-E — projective centrum cross-frame and dual wings

Let
\[
K=(1,1,1),\qquad e_+=(0,1,1),\qquad e_-=(0,1,-1).
\]
Every point `X=(A,z,w)` has the unique decomposition
\[
X=A K+\xi e_++\eta e_-,
\qquad
\xi=\frac{z+w-2A}{2},\quad \eta=\frac{z-w}{2}.
\]

For
\[
U(A,z,w)=(A,2A-z,2A-w)
\]
one has
\[
\boxed{U(A,\xi,\eta)=(A,-\xi,-\eta).}
\]
Thus `K` is the affine/projective centrum and the full wing coordinate is reversed.

## Exact frame

\[
R=K+e_+=[1:2:2],\qquad E=K-e_+=[1:0:0],
\]
\[
I_+=[e_+]=[0:1:1],\qquad O=[e_-]=[0:1:-1].
\]

- `R,E` are opposite finite points on the symmetric wing;
- `I_+` is that wing's point at infinity;
- the elliptic origin `O` is the antisymmetric point at infinity;
- `U` fixes `K,I_+,O` projectively and swaps `R,E`;
- `(R,E;K,I_+)=-1`.

## Dual chambers

The chamber
\[
0<w<z<A/5
\]
is sent exactly to
\[
9A/5<C<D<2A,
\qquad C=2A-z,\ D=2A-w.
\]
The two chambers are opposite affine wings about `K`.

## Boundary

Three origin-like structures remain distinct:

1. the excluded lattice origin generating projective rays;
2. the symmetry centrum `K`;
3. the elliptic group origin `O=I_-` at antisymmetric infinity.

The cross-frame relates them but does not identify them ontologically.
