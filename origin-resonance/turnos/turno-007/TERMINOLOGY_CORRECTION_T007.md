# Terminology correction — superaccess versus hyperaccess

The information-theoretic construction introduced in Turno 006 is retained, but its name is corrected.

## Superaccess

Superaccess is access to the hidden internal/subsystemic information omitted by an initial observation. Its exact RGΦ observables are conditional entropies of the local receipt history and terminal holonomy.

## Hyperaccess

Hyperaccess is not recovery of all hidden information. It is a qualitatively small completion key situated at a nesting boundary. The key resolves a phase/branch ambiguity and synchronizes an almost complete internal description with the external completion.

A hyperaccess key can therefore have vanishing normalized size while producing an unbounded change in the completed lift.

The old file `RELATIVE_HYPERACCESS_T006.md` is retained for provenance and is superseded terminologically by the superaccess theorem in Turno 007.
