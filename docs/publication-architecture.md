# Publication architecture

RGΦ is too broad for a single manuscript. The publication strategy separates mature theorems from research lines that still require generalization.

## Paper I — Nonnested extremal targets in a rational radix–Möbius semigroup

### Scientific thesis

In an exact noncommutative rational semigroup generated by a radix map and a Möbius-conjugate branch, earliest hits of successively deeper extremal targets need not form a nested sequence of optimal precursors.

### Core contents

- base-\(B\) formal setup;
- decimal benchmark;
- exact \(\tau_1,\ldots,\tau_4\);
- three consecutive nonnesting counterexamples;
- failure of scalar portal height as a sufficient value summary;
- exact \(\kappa\)-robustness intervals;
- computer-assisted proof protocol.

### Explicit exclusions

Paper I does not require:

- \(\tau_5=212\);
- full trap classification;
- full hidden-state theorem;
- global orthomodular formalization;
- Artin/Kummer asymptotics.

### Recommended venues

Primary targets:

1. **Journal of Modern Dynamics**
2. **Experimental Mathematics**

A more ambitious dynamics venue becomes realistic only after broader family-level theorems are added.

## Paper II — Farey shadows and hidden \(p\)-adic control

### Scientific thesis

Visible rational state and scalar extremal height can be insufficient for future control; arithmetic context carried by Farey/\(p\)-adic coordinates can determine divergent optimal actions.

### Required upgrade before submission

- minimal formal hidden state;
- theorem beyond isolated witness pairs;
- comparison with natural extensions, symbolic codings, and \(p\)-adic dynamics;
- independent reproducibility.

### Candidate venues

- Journal of Modern Dynamics
- Journal of Number Theory
- Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems, if generalized substantially

## Paper III — Finite modular traps and extremal escape

### Scientific thesis

Finite no-escape structures in the rational semigroup arise from modular radix skeletons coupled by the Möbius branch, yielding exact infinite Bellman distances for entire basins.

### Required upgrade

- infinite-family trap theorem;
- basin theorem or nontrivial basin lower bounds;
- classification in general base \(B\).

### Candidate venues

- Journal of Modern Dynamics
- Nonlinearity
- Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems after major generalization

## Computational companion

### Scope

- exact search architecture;
- mathematically justified pruning;
- canonical encodings;
- independent engines;
- certificate checking;
- reproducible datasets.

### Candidate venues

- Experimental Mathematics
- Journal of Computational Dynamics
- Mathematics of Computation if the computational method becomes independently general.

## Separate arithmetic line

The Artin/Kummer-style density program should remain separate from the portal/Bellman manuscripts unless a theorem creates a direct mathematical dependency.

## Preprint strategy

Canonical sequence:

1. GitHub public research compendium;
2. Zenodo archival DOI for frozen software/data release;
3. arXiv for the manuscript, primary category math.DS and likely cross-list math.NT;
4. HAL as a secondary open archive;
5. journal submission.

## Editorial rule

One manuscript should answer one central mathematical question.

Repository breadth is an advantage; manuscript breadth is not.
