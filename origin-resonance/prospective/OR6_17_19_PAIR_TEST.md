# OR-6P — prospective follow-up to the post-hoc 17/19 split

The discovery of `n=17` is already observed and is not prospective. The following endpoints are frozen now for future canonical computations.

## Primary endpoint — full rank profile

Compute a verified Mordell–Weil rank interval for every

\[
F_n,
\qquad n=1,\ldots,19.
\]

A strong hit occurs iff `n=17` is the unique index with positive rank and a rational point in the strict chamber.

Any other positive-rank chamber curve weakens uniqueness and must be reported.

## Secondary endpoint — envelope separation

Determine whether `F_19` has a rational point in the strict chamber.

- no rational chamber point: supports the split `17 arithmetic / 19 envelope`;
- rational chamber point: weakens that split;
- RGΦ-admissible point: decisively refutes a purely real-envelope role.

## Tertiary endpoint — first full RGΦ realization

Among all nineteen curves, record the least and the first canonically ordered index admitting a point satisfying simultaneously:

- the cubic equation;
- the strict chamber;
- `A | z^3+w^3`;
- `gcd(C,D)=1`;
- all primitive-state conditions.

A hit at 17 supports the pair recurrence. A hit elsewhere weakens it.

## Anti-selection rule

All nineteen curves must be adjudicated with the same method and stopping criteria. The process may not stop when curve 17 succeeds.
