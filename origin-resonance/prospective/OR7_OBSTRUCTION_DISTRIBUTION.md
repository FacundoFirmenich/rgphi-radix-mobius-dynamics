# OR-7P — obstruction distribution along rational chamber points

For every newly certified primitive rational chamber point on any `F_n`, record

\[
d=\gcd(2A-z,2A-w)
\]

and the reduced denominators of `f/D` and `g/C`.

The defect-completion theorem guarantees that both denominators divide `d`.

The prospectively tested refinements are:

1. **MAXIMAL_DEFECT:** both denominators equal `d`;
2. **COPRIME_HIT:** `d=1`, which automatically closes both cycle divisibilities;
3. empirical distribution of prime factors of `d` under a fixed canonical ordering of rational points.

No particular prime, including 23 or 29, is predicted.
