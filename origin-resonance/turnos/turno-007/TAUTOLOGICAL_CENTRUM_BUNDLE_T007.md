# Tautological projective bundle and the three centrum roles

For a real vector space `V_d`, the projection

\[
V_d\setminus\{0\}\to\mathbb P(V_d)
\]

is the nonzero part of the tautological line bundle. A projective state is a ray; radial scale lives in its fibre and the zero vector is excluded from the projective base.

For `d=2`, primitive lattice representatives select an integral section over rational rays. The content cover enlarges the fibre to the `B`-smooth scaling monoid and reconstructs the unnormalized matrix orbit.

For `d=3`, the elliptic pencil lives in `P(V_3)`. The linear involution `U(A,z,w)=(A,2A-z,2A-w)` lifts to the tautological bundle and reverses both centred wing coordinates.

The theory distinguishes:

1. the excluded zero vector, which generates rays;
2. the projective centrum `K=[1:1:1]` of the involution;
3. the elliptic group origin `O=[0:1:-1]`.

They share a projectivization architecture but are not the same point. No canonical cross-dimensional morphism between the two tautological bundles has yet been proved.
