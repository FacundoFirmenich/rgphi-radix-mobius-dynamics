\[
\lambda_n=\frac{\pi}{6}10^n.
\]

\[
C_n=10\lambda_n.
\]

\[
\boxed{a_n=\frac{2}{30}C_n=\frac{\pi}{9}10^n.}
\]

Por tanto

\[
\boxed{U_{n,\sigma}=\frac{\pi}{9}10^n(15+\sigma).}
\]

\[
\boxed{[14:15:16].}
\]

La supernormalización es

\[
\boxed{(14/15,1,16/15).}
\]
