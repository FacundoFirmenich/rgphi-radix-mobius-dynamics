# Paper I — one-page specialist brief

## Result

In a partial rational semigroup generated by an integer-radix map and its
Möbius conjugate, the states that first attain one extremal target need not be
the best precursors of the next target.

For `B=10`, seed `17/19`, `κ=11`, and the certified 23-state terminal
convention:

\[
(\tau_1,\tau_2,\tau_3,\tau_4)=(45,85,120,162).
\]

Continuing the first-hit states gives total arrival times

\[
87>85,\qquad139>120,\qquad168>162.
\]

The chronology is unchanged on

\[
\kappa\in[10.6197,11.643),
\]

and all three nonnesting inequalities persist simultaneously on

\[
\kappa\in[10.6197,11.01).
\]

## Why it matters

The result separates three notions that can coincide in simpler stopping
problems:

1. first attainment of the current target;
2. maximum present extremal height;
3. minimum future cost to a deeper target.

Consequently, elapsed time plus attained target depth is not a Bellman-sufficient
compressed state, and current height is not a value function for the sequential
portal objective.
