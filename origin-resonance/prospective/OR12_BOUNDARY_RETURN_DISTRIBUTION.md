# OR-12P — prospective distribution of boundary returns

For every future certified chamber multiple `mP` on `F_17` and every prime `p` dividing

\[
\gcd(2A_m-z_m,2A_m-w_m),
\]

record uniformly:

1. whether `[m]P mod p = R=[1:2:2]`;
2. `#F_17(F_p)` and the Frobenius trace;
3. the order of `P mod p`;
4. the discrete logarithm of `R` in `<P>`, when defined;
5. whether `2m=#F_17(F_p)-1`;
6. the cubic divisibility defect.

No prime, multiplier or group order is predicted. The Round-021 tuple

\[
(m,p,#E)=(44,83,89)
\]

is the frozen regression case.
