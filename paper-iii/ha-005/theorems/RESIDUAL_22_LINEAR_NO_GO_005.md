# HA-005-B — exact closure of the 22 residual profiles

The 22 HA-004 profiles without an individually unit fibre component all contain, in their `Q_0` fibre, a cancellable linear divisibility

\[
Q_0\mid L(P).
\]

Primitiveness makes the cancelled monomial factor invertible modulo `Q_0`.

## Fifteen strict-strip contradictions

For fifteen profiles, exact nonnegative combinations of the strict high forms prove

\[
-Q_0<L(P)<0.
\]

No integer in that open interval is divisible by `Q_0`; hence these profiles are impossible. The three linear forms are

\[
P_0-2P_2,
\qquad
P_0-2P_1,
\qquad
3P_0-2P_1.
\]

## Seven forced-zero contradictions

For the remaining seven profiles, exact nonnegative combinations prove

\[
-Q_0<L(P)<Q_0.
\]

Divisibility therefore forces `L(P)=0`. In every case that equality makes the numerator of state 0 even while its denominator is `2Q_0`; the state is not primitive.
