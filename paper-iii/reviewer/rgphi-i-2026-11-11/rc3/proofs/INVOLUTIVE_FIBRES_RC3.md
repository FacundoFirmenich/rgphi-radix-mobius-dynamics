# Two-cycles as modular involutions

An unreduced `T` two-cycle in denominator fibre `q` forces `B^2=1 mod q`. An unreduced `S` two-cycle in numerator fibre `p` forces `B^2=1 mod p` on complementary coordinates.

The exact five-state realization uses the four nontrivial involutions

- `171^2 = 1 mod 731`,
- `254^2 = 1 mod 1265`,
- `349^2 = 1 mod 406`,
- `179^2 = 1 mod 712`.

The sixth historical congruence is redundant: `B=1161 mod 1218` already implies `B=349 mod 406`.
