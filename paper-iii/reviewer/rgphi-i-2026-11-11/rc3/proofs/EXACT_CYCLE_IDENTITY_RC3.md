# Exact content identity on directed cycles

For a directed cycle `C`, let `R_C` be the product of all local content defects. Then

`R_C = product_{T-edges i} x_{i+1}/x_i = product_{S-edges i} x_i/x_{i+1}`.

On a `T` edge with defect `g_i`, `p_{i+1}/p_i=(x_{i+1}/x_i)/g_i`; on an `S` edge with defect `h_i`, `p_{i+1}/p_i=1/h_i`. Multiplication around the cycle gives the first identity; the product of all state ratios is one, giving the second.

The high-cycle budget follows immediately:

`R_C <= rho_C^{m_T}` and `R_C <= rho_C^{m_S}`.
