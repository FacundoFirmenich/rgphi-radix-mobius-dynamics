# Scientific niche — RC3

The project studies **finite arithmetic subdynamics in a coupled piecewise-projective semigroup**.

A realization must satisfy simultaneously:

1. a finite labelled transformation graph;
2. primitive projective coordinates;
3. exact local content/gcd data;
4. a common integer base satisfying all branch congruences.

The paper contributes:

- a determinant-controlled primitive-content formalism;
- exact cycle identities and obstructions;
- a sharp permutation threshold at cardinality five;
- exact fixed-base bounded-cardinality decidability;
- finite combinatorial types plus CRT base fibres.

It does not claim general Möbius coding, ergodicity, complete five-state realization, minimality of the displayed base, or physical interpretation.
