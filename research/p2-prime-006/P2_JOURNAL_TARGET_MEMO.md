# P2 journal-target adjudication

## Primary: Journal of Modern Dynamics
Best current fit because P2 is arithmetic dynamics at the interface with number theory. Keep mathematics/theorem architecture primary and computer-assisted benchmarks secondary.

## Stretch: Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems
Excellent thematic fit, but the current paper lacks a global ergodic/natural-extension/invariant-measure theorem. Consider only if that changes materially.

## Arithmetic alternative: Journal of Number Theory
Strong fit for primitive-vector arithmetic, composite CRT and prime-wise reduction. Frame Bellman/control as a consequence of the arithmetic transducer.

## Reserve: Nonlinearity
Broadly compatible, but less targeted than JMD/JNT.

**Decision:** freeze style and cover materials for JMD; do not submit before specialist review and institutional citation chaining close.
