# P2 state-reduction literature bridge

Let \(\widehat X\) be the lifted arithmetic state and \(\pi:\widehat X\to X\) the visible-rational projection. For a declared task \(G\) and horizon \(H\), P2 computes exact behavioral equivalence classes by merging lifted states only when their task-relevant future behavior agrees over the declared horizon.

## Relation to lumpability
Classical lumpability asks when a partition of a Markov chain preserves a Markov process after aggregation. P2 is deterministic and task-conditioned. Therefore its finite quotients should be called **task-relative behavioral quotients**, not global lumpings, unless Markov preservation is separately proved.

## Relation to predictive/causal states
Computational mechanics groups histories by identical conditional distributions over all futures. P2's equivalence is narrower: a declared arithmetic task and finite future family. The analogy is useful for positioning, not a priority claim.

## Recommended manuscript wording

> The lifted state admits task-dependent quotients. These quotients are related in spirit to state aggregation, lumpability, predictive-state equivalence and automata minimization, but our claims are narrower: for a declared arithmetic task and finite horizon we determine exactly which Farey-shadow phase distinctions may be merged without altering the relevant future behavior. We do not claim global Markov lumpability or a universal minimal predictive representation.

## Novelty boundary
Do not claim novelty for state aggregation, sufficient statistics, lumpability, causal states, predictive states or Myhill–Nerode equivalence. The contribution is the exact arithmetic realization and solvable quotient structure generated by primitive reduction in the radix–Möbius/Farey-shadow system.
