# Literature update — 2026 Q4

This update records sources verified after the initial literature matrix was assembled.

## Projective/Farey classification

Giovanni Panti, **“There is only one Farey map”**, *Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata* (2026), DOI `10.1007/s10231-026-01693-1`.

Relevance: a high-priority comparison for any broad classification language involving two-branch projective Farey systems. RGΦ should claim only its specific partial semigroup and extremal theorem, not a new general Farey classification.

## Contracted Farey expansions and natural extensions

Karma Dajani, Cor Kraaikamp, and Slade Sanderson, **“Inducing contractions of the mother of all continued fractions”**, *Journal of Number Theory* 278 (2026), 816–874.

Relevance: close structural precedent for coupling inducing/contraction mechanisms in Farey natural extensions. The RGΦ distinction lies in exact first-hit optimization and its partial radix–Möbius action.

## First-return systems

Thomas A. Schmidt, **“First return systems for some continued fraction maps”**, arXiv:2511.12835 (2025).

Relevance: high-value comparison for any return-section interpretation of RGΦ trajectories. It does not obviously state the nonnested exact first-hit theorem.

## Finite rational trajectories

Valérie Berthé, Stephen Cantrell, Jungwon Lee, and Mark Pollicott, **“Multidimensional statistics for finite orbits of generalised continued fractions”**, arXiv:2507.11295 (2025).

Relevance: demonstrates that finite rational trajectories are an active and theoretically meaningful object. RGΦ uses finite trajectories for exact optimization rather than statistical approximation.

## Optimal continued-fraction terminology

Slade Sanderson, **“Superoptimal continued fractions”**, arXiv:2508.19743 (2025).

Relevance: confirms that “optimal” and “superoptimal” already have specialized approximation-theoretic meanings. Paper I should retain the more precise phrase “first-hit optimality” and avoid implying connection to best-approximation optimal continued fractions.

## p-adic Möbius transformations

Giuliano Romeo and Giulia Salvatori, **“The arithmetic of continued fractions in the field of p-adic numbers”**, arXiv:2512.11069 (2025).

Relevance: high-priority comparison for the future hidden-state paper because it studies Möbius transformations of p-adic continued fractions and information loss under transformed expansions.

## Updated literature verdict

No source in this update states an evidently equivalent robust nonnested first-hit theorem for the RGΦ benchmark system. The priority claim remains provisional until specialist review and closed-database citation chaining are complete.
