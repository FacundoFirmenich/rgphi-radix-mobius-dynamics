# Code of Conduct

This project follows a concise research conduct standard:

- criticize claims and methods, not people;
- report uncertainty and negative results explicitly;
- do not fabricate data, citations, computations, or provenance;
- preserve authorship, priority, and contribution records;
- disclose conflicts of interest;
- keep discussions technically focused and respectful.

Serious misconduct may be reported privately to f.firmenich@cedesur.org.
