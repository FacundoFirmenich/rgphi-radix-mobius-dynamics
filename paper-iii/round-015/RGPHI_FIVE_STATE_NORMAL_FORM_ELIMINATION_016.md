# RGΦ Five-State Normal-Form Elimination 016

Input: the certified defect-one Diophantine normal forms from Round 015.

For each form:

1. expand the radix congruences on its numerator/denominator fibres;
2. derive cycle divisibilities \(Q\mid B^m-1\) and \(P\mid B^n-1\);
3. combine them with the strict congruence and gcd conditions;
4. quotient forms sharing the same polynomial/divisibility system;
5. eliminate globally or construct a complete exact counterfamily.

No return to denominator-bounded enumeration is authorized.
