1. Treat
   \[
   \mathcal I(w)=\log\Delta_w
   \]
   as the exact additive receipt of radial content removed by primitive projection.
2. Determine whether a normalized functional
   \[
   \alpha(w)=\mathcal I(w)/(|w|\log B)
   \]
   satisfies a useful monotonicity, comparison theorem or decision-theoretic interpretation.
3. Do not identify it with the broader hyperaccess programme without such a theorem.

## B. Helical monodromy tests

For every canonical finite cycle record:

- cycle length;
- defect product `Delta`;
- log-radial pitch `log Delta`;
- normalized share `alpha`;
- whether `Delta=1` or `Delta>1`.

Test whether nonpermutation rupture is characterized by nonzero monodromy or whether permutation cycles may also carry `Delta>1`.
