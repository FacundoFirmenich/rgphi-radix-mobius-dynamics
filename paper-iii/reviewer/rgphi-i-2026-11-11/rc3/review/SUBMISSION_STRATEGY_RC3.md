# Submission strategy — RC3

## Core pitch

Finite rational subdynamics can be studied as arithmetic transformation graphs on primitive projective rays. Exact content loss supplies a cycle calculus. For the coupled radix–Möbius system this yields a sharp permutation threshold: bijective through four states, first exact failure at five. At fixed base and cardinality the entire problem is decidable in an explicit finite divisor universe.

## Strongest journal-facing features

1. memorable sharp theorem;
2. complete symbolic proof with a hand-verifiable final modular branch;
3. general determinant-content and fixed-base decision results;
4. explicit exact realization organized by modular involutions and CRT;
5. unusually clean reproducibility without substituting computation for proof;
6. precise technical placement among Möbius number systems, radix dynamics and arithmetic dynamics.

## Recommended order

- **Stretch:** IMRN or Transactions of the AMS after two strong external mathematical reports.
- **High-fit:** Nonlinearity, Journal of Number Theory, Proceedings of the AMS (if compressed), Experimental Mathematics.
- **Dynamics route:** ETDS only if editors value the finite arithmetic-dynamics theorem despite the absence of measure/ergodic results.

## Generalist-elite boundary

RC3 is written at a generalist standard of proof and exposition. It does not yet contain the breadth/depth usually needed for Annals, Acta, Inventiones or JEMS. The correct route to that category is not rhetorical escalation; it is a companion theorem on global realizability, five-state arithmetic classification or a general family under arbitrary unimodular conjugates.
