# Literature update — RC3

The manuscript's technical positioning now explicitly distinguishes:

- Möbius number systems: Kůrka (2009, 2012, 2016), Kůrka–Kazda (2010);
- piecewise Möbius ergodicity/natural extensions: Calta–Kraaikamp–Schmidt (2024); structural stability: Leriche–Sienra (2025 preprint);
- rational points in multiplication-invariant/self-similar sets: Li–Li–Wu (2023), Kong–Li–Wang (2025), Li–Wang–Zhao (2025);
- shift radix systems: Kirschenhofer–Thuswaldner survey;
- rational-base numeration: Akiyama–Frougny–Sakarovitch (2008);
- arithmetic dynamics: Silverman; Benedetto et al.

Novelty is delimited to the coupled radix/conjugate finite-component problem, primitive content calculus, sharp threshold and effective recognition results.
