# Fibre injectivity and the Schreier regime

- If `gcd(q,B)=1`, the radix map on reduced states with denominator `q` acts on numerators by multiplication by `B mod q`; it is injective.
- If `gcd(p,B)=1`, the conjugate map on reduced high states with numerator `p` acts on complements `c=q-p` by multiplication by `B mod p`; it is injective.

Hence, if every edge in a finite closed component has defect one, both generators are permutations. A strongly connected defect-free component is the Schreier graph of a transitive action of the free group on two generators. Strict content loss is the arithmetic mechanism that moves the action into the transformation-semigroup regime.
