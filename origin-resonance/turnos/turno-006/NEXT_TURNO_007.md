# Origin–Resonance — Turno 007

## A. Holonomy Smith normal form

Compute Smith normal forms of the prime-valued cycle maps for every canonical finite component available, including torsion quotients and equal-length alias lattices.

## B. Hyperaccess channel theorem

1. Develop the observer hierarchy from endpoint to path to full lift.
2. Determine zero-error and probabilistic capacities over long paths.
3. Test whether path-receipt entropy rate is positive under natural stationary policies.
4. Preserve the distinction between terminal holonomy and full receipt history.

## C. Six-state arithmetic realization

Work on the `2160` complete marked skeletons, stratified by
\[
TTTSSS,\quad TTSTSS,\quad TSTSTS
\]
and defects `4..7`. Add fibre partitions, exact gcd labels, cycle spectral square tests and CRT compatibility. Priority: the alternating `TSTSTS` jurisdiction.

## D. Centrum bundle

Construct the projective surface/bundle containing centred wing coordinates, fixed axis, elliptic pencil sections and content cover. Do not identify spaces without an explicit morphism.

## E. Elliptic relation lattice

Use good-reduction data and height methods to determine the subgroup generated by `P` and `R`. The theorem `R notin <P>` is frozen. Study the nonzero point `Q=2R+P` and its reduction kernels.

## F. Asymptotic access geometry

Compute support functions, extreme cycles and policy-optimal rates for every newly realized component. Relate spectral cycle eigenvalues to access-rate extremals.
