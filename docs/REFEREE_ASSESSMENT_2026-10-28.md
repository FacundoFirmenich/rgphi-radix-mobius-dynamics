## Overall recommendation

**Major revision toward a multi-paper publication program; Paper I is near external-review readiness.**

The project contains genuine mathematics, unusually disciplined exact computation and a credible research program. Its strongest current contribution is not any single ingredient—radix maps, Möbius maps, Farey geometry, p-adic bookkeeping, dynamic programming or finite graph analysis—but their exact coupling in a controlled rational system.

The program is already stronger than a numerical curiosity. It is not yet a completed general theory.

## Major strengths

1. Exact arithmetic is used throughout the canonical claims.
2. The nonnesting phenomenon is robust on an explicit threshold interval.
3. The project distinguishes symbolic proof, finite exhaustion, large computer assistance and open claims.
4. Trap discovery has been converted into exact non-escape statements in concrete cases.
5. The Stage-A global cut is a strategically superior reduction to serial branch closure.
6. The public research object is unusually rich for an early-stage mathematical program.
7. Experimental branches are governed by explicit authority boundaries.

## Major concerns

### 1. The main computer-assisted claim is not yet independently auditable end to end

The time-185 frontier is identified by hashes, but the raw payload is unavailable. A referee cannot recompute the Stage-B2 continuation or even revalidate the original frontier without those bytes or a full regeneration protocol.

**Required correction:** archive the payloads, or regenerate them from a frozen earlier state with a public transcript and independent checker.

### 2. A bounded trap atlas is not a global theorem

The denominator-\(\le500\) census is valuable evidence, but it does not classify all traps.

**Required correction:** prove arithmetic sufficient conditions for infinite trap families and a basin theorem.

### 3. The hidden-state program needs a minimality theorem

Farey defects and prime-wise reduction ledgers clearly matter. The present evidence does not yet identify a minimal Markov-complete state or prove that a proposed augmentation is sufficient.

**Required correction:** formulate state-equivalence under all future controls and prove upper/lower bounds on required information.

### 4. The novelty claim must remain compositional

Recent work covers open beta maps, projective/Farey algorithms, piecewise Möbius stability, p-adic finite-state interpreters and ergodic optimization. RGΦ should claim a new exact interaction and theorem package, not ownership of these parent fields.

### 5. Generalization should be theorem-led

Multibase holdouts are useful, but external generalization will remain below top-tier level until a general symbolic theorem explains regime changes.

## Journal-specific advice

- **ETDS:** lead with a theorem of dynamical significance, not a computation log.
- **Nonlinearity:** articulate the mechanism and robustness across a parameter cell.
- **Mathematics of Computation:** complete Stage B2, expose a small checker, and show the method is reusable.
- **TOMS:** present software methodology of lasting value, with failure/restart semantics and benchmarks.
- **JSC:** foreground exact symbolic algorithms, correctness and complexity.
- **ReScience C:** appropriate only for an independent team replicating a published RGΦ paper; self-replication is outside its policy.

## Decision

The project merits serious specialist review. The appropriate posture is neither self-dismissal nor triumphalism: the exact results are real, the research object is unusually mature, and the remaining gaps are sharply identifiable.
