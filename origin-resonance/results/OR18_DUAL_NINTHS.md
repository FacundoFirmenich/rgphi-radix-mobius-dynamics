# OR-18 — dual exact one-ninth boundaries

Use normalized chamber variables

\[
a=A/z>5,
\qquad
t=w/z\in(0,1).
\]

## Boundary-slope excess

In the Round-022 boundary coordinates,

\[
\frac DC
=
\frac{2a-t}{2a-1}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\frac DC-1
=
\frac{1-t}{2a-1}.
\]

Hence

\[
0<\frac DC-1<\frac19,
\]

and

\[
\boxed{\sup(D/C-1)=1/9}
\]

as `a->5+`, `t->0+`.

## Negative h-branch

For `2/3<t<1`,

\[
-\frac hz
=
\frac{(3t-2)(2-t)}{2a-t}.
\]

Since `a>5`,

\[
-\frac hz
<
\frac{(3t-2)(2-t)}{10-t}
<
\frac19.
\]

The last inequality is equivalent to

\[
27t^2-73t+46>0
=
(t-1)(27t-46)>0
\]

for `2/3<t<1`. The supremum `1/9` is approached as `a->5+`, `t->1-`.

Thus two distinct normalized observables have the same exact sharp endpoint:

\[
\boxed{\sup(D/C-1)=\sup(-h/z)_+=1/9}.
\]

They are realized at opposite ends of the chamber parameter `t`.

## Boundary
