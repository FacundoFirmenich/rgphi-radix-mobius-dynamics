# HA-006 — complete six-state and effective arithmetic gate

## Binding consequence of HA-005

The exactly-two-strict rank-two jurisdiction with factors `2` and `3` is globally impossible. Any six-state rank-two realization must therefore leave that jurisdiction through at least one of:

- a defect in `{4,5,6,7}`;
- three or more strict edges globally;
- independent valuation directions supported on distinct simple-cycle architectures;
- another decorated type not represented by the HA-004 minimal catalogue.

## Front A — complete six-state decorated catalogue

1. Enumerate all strongly connected pairs of endomaps on six vertices up to labelled isomorphism and generator exchange.
2. Label strict edges by defects `<8` subject to every simple-cycle budget.
3. Compute valuation-cycle rank and Smith data.
4. Apply forced-collision, high-cone, component-Nullstellensatz and spectral pruning before arithmetic realization.
5. No nonexistence claim before complete decorated coverage.

## Front B — effective relative-integral points

For all nineteen `DF015-0015` cubics:

1. construct and verify birational maps to minimal Weierstrass models;
2. compute saturated Mordell-Weil groups where feasible;
3. enumerate points integral relative to the double base point `R=[1:2:2]`;
4. use the full two-adic theorem and the HA-005 three-modulus factor splitting;
5. map every point back to `(A,C,D)` and the original RGΦ states.

## Front C — dense five-state branches

Use divisor allocation plus exact modular/spectral fingerprints for `0003`, `0007-B1`, `0011`, and `0017`. Promote only a global elimination or an exact state tuple followed by the universal CRT theorem.

## Front D — countergraph uniqueness

Turn the current finite uniqueness jurisdiction into a descent, a finite divisor-allocation theorem, or a parametric family. A higher cutoff is secondary evidence only.

## Passage criterion

HA-006 passes with at least one of:

- a complete six-state decorated catalogue with arithmetic adjudication of a nontrivial stratum;
- an exact six-state rank-two realization outside the minimal jurisdiction;
- effective closure or realization of `DF015-0015`;
- a new exact five-state realization or global elimination of an open form;
- a global uniqueness theorem or parametric family for the known countergraph tuple.
