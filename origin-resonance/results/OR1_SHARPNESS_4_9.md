# OR-1 result — `4/9` is not sharp

Put

\[
a=A/z>5,\qquad t=w/z\in(0,1).
\]

Then

\[
\frac hz=\frac{(2-3t)(2-t)}{2a-t}.
\]

For `0<t<2/3`,

\[
\frac hz<\frac{4-8t+3t^2}{10-t}<\frac25,
\]

because

\[
5(4-8t+3t^2)<2(10-t)
\iff t(15t-38)<0.
\]

For `2/3<t<1`,

\[
\frac{|h|}{z}<\frac{-4+8t-3t^2}{10-t}<\frac25,
\]

because `15t^2-42t+40>0` has negative discriminant.

Taking `a -> 5+` and `t -> 0+` gives

\[
\frac{|h|}{z}\longrightarrow\frac4{10}=\frac25.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\sup |h|/z=2/5}.
\]

The previously used `4/9` is a valid but non-sharp proof bound.
