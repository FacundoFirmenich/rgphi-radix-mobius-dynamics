## Title

Primitive projective radix–Möbius dynamics: content cocycles and a sharp rigidity threshold

## Author

Facundo Firmenich

## Categories

- Primary: `math.DS`
- Cross-list: `math.NT`

## Abstract

Fix an integer base B≥2 and consider the rational maps T_B(x)={Bx} and S_B=Φ∘T_B∘Φ^{-1}, where Φ(x)=1/(1+x). We introduce an exact primitive projective lift of this partial semigroup to coprime lattice vectors. Each branch is represented by an integer matrix of determinant B, followed by primitive normalization; the removed content is an integer cocycle whose local values are gcd(q,B) for T_B and gcd(p,B) for S_B at a reduced state p/q. On a periodic word, the content product is an eigenvalue of the branch-matrix product, yielding an exact trace identity and a square-discriminant obstruction.

We study finite closed strongly connected components contained in the strict high domain (1/2,1). A cycle budget bounds the total content loss by the numerical spread of the cycle and gives general large-prime and short-diameter rigidity criteria. We prove that every such component of cardinality at most four is permutational under both generators. We then construct an explicit five-state component, valid for an infinite arithmetic progression of bases, for which one generator is not a permutation. Thus the least possible nonpermutational cardinality is exactly five. Finally, we prove that each fixed cardinality decomposes into finitely many defect-decorated combinatorial types and that, for a fixed primitive state tuple, the realizing bases are either empty or one complete arithmetic progression. The proofs are symbolic except for one finite exact nonsquare certificate, supplied with independent checkers.
