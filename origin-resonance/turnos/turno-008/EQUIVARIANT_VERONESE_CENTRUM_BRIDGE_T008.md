# Equivariant quadratic bridge between the projective line and centrum plane

Define the projective-line involution

\[
j([x:y])=[-y:x]
\]

and the centrum involution

\[
U[A:z:w]=[A:2A-z:2A-w].
\]

The integral quadratic embedding

\[
\nu([x:y])=[x^2+y^2:2x^2+xy:2x^2-xy]
\]

satisfies

\[
\boxed{U\circ\nu=\nu\circ j}.
\]

It is the standard Veronese map followed by

\[
L=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1\\2&1&0\\2&-1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad\det L=-4.
\]

Its image is the invariant conic

\[
(z+w)^2+4(z-w)^2=4A(z+w).
\]

The quadratic basis gives the exact centrum dictionary

\[
L(x^2)=R=[1:2:2],\quad
L(y^2)=E=[1:0:0],\quad
L(xy)=O=[0:1:-1],
\]

and

\[
L(x^2+y^2)=R+E=2K,
\qquad K=[1:1:1].
\]

Thus the pure squares are the opposite finite wings, their sum is the centrum, and the mixed term is the antisymmetric projective origin. At bundle level,

\[
\nu^*\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}(-1)\cong\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-2).
\]

This is an explicit arithmetic morphism, not an identification of the two state spaces.
