\[
T_B(x)=\{Bx\},\qquad S_B=\Phi T_B\Phi^{-1},\qquad \Phi(x)=\frac1{1+x}.

- both restricted generators are permutations through cardinality four;
- an explicit nonpermutational five-state component exists for every base in one infinite arithmetic progression;
- hence the least nonpermutation cardinality is exactly five.
