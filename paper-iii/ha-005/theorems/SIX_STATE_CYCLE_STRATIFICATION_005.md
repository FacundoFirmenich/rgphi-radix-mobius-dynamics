# HA-005-D — universal six-state cycle stratification

For any simple high cycle in a six-state component,

\[
R_C<2^{\min(m_T,m_S)},
\qquad
\min(m_T,m_S)\le3.
\]

Consequences:

1. A simple cycle contains at most two strict edges. Three strict factors have product at least `8`, contradicting the strict bound `<8`.
2. If a simple cycle contains two strict edges, it must be a Hamiltonian balanced `3T/3S` cycle. Their defect pair is necessarily `(2,2)` or `(2,3)`.
3. A single defect in `{4,5,6,7}` can occur only on a Hamiltonian balanced `3T/3S` cycle.
4. A simple cycle that carries two independent prime directions can only carry factors `2` and `3`.

This theorem does not imply that all rank-two components belong to the HA-004 jurisdiction: independent directions may live on different simple cycles, and additional strict edges may occur globally.
