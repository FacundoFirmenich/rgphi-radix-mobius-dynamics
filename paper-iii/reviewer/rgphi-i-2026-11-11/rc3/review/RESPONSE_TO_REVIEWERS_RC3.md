# Integrated response to adversarial reviews — RC3

## Decision

RC3 treats every review item as one of four classes:

1. **mathematical gap** — repaired in the proof;
2. **expository gap** — made explicit in the article or supplement;
3. **strategic-context gap** — repaired through a technical literature comparison and narrower claim language;
4. **scope-expansion request** — accepted only when it yields a complete theorem within the paper; otherwise identified as companion work.

No criticism is answered by rhetoric alone.

## Decisive mathematical changes

### 1. General projective-content theorem

For primitive `v` and integer `M`, `cont(Mv)` divides `|det M|`; content is invariant under unimodular coordinate changes. This embeds the local gcd laws in a general arithmetic statement and clarifies the relation with homogeneous lifts/resultants.

### 2. Fibre injectivity and the Schreier regime

The paper now proves injectivity of `T_B` on primitive denominator fibres and of `S_B` on primitive numerator fibres. A finite defect-free component is therefore a transitive two-generator permutation action, equivalently a Schreier graph. Strict content loss is the exact mechanism by which the system enters the transformation-semigroup regime.

### 3. Exact cycle identity

The total defect on a cycle is exactly the product of state ratios along either letter class. The cycle budget becomes an immediate corollary and no longer appears as an isolated telescoping trick.

### 4. Fully auditable four-state proof

The origins of every decisive divisibility are written out. The quotient bounds use uniform factored inequalities. The exceptional branch has the sharp range `1<=j<=18` and a hand-checkable modular proof. The historical 37-row certificate is no longer a premise.

### 5. Modular design of the five-state object

The counterexample is reinterpreted through nontrivial square roots of unity in primitive fibres. Only five CRT congruences are independent. Exact gcd constancy on the full base progression is proved.

### 6. Fixed-base bounded-cardinality decision theorem

For every fixed `(B,n)`, all candidate numerators and denominators divide an explicit integer `N_{B,n}`. This yields an exact finite decision procedure. The theorem is general in both base and cardinality; no efficiency claim is made.

### 7. Exact small-base census

The decision theorem was executed for all `2<=B<=60` and `n<=5`. The exact census examines 2,210,941 rational candidates, finds 14 closed SCCs, and no nonpermutation component. This is finite evidence and a lower bound on the least realizing base, not a global minimality theorem.

## Strategic repositioning

The manuscript now identifies its niche as:

> **finite arithmetic subdynamics in a coupled piecewise-projective semigroup**.

The object requires simultaneous realization of a labelled transformation graph, primitive projective coordinates and exact divisibility constraints. The paper contributes the first sharp bijectivity threshold in this niche, an exact cycle calculus, and effective recognition at fixed base/cardinality.

A technical comparison table distinguishes the work from:

- Möbius number systems and sofic expansion languages;
- piecewise Möbius ergodic theory;
- rational points in `x b`-invariant/self-similar sets;
- shift radix systems and rational-base numeration;
- general arithmetic dynamics.

## What RC3 deliberately does not claim

- complete arithmetic classification of all five-state types;
- minimality of the displayed very large base;
- existence for every base or every cardinality `n>=5`;
- an ergodic, pressure or Lyapunov theory;
- a physical model;
- priority over Möbius number systems, radix dynamics or projective matrix codings.

## Editorial status

RC3 is internally submission-ready as a mathematically complete paper with a transparent scope. Journal selectivity still depends on external assessment of significance. Resolving every proof/exposition issue cannot manufacture the cross-field importance demanded by Annals/Inventiones/JEMS; RC3 instead maximizes the paper's actual contribution without inflating it.
