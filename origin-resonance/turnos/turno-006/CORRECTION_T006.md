# Turno 006 correction

Turno 005 stated that the prime-resolved vector could retain exact information lost by the scalar normalized share. For fixed base and known length this is incorrect in exact arithmetic: unique factorization makes `nu`, `Delta`, `log Delta`, and `alpha` mutually determining.

The vector remains useful as a coordinate decomposition and under finite precision, but the rigorous information gain arises only relative to an observer that omits the receipt or its history. The observer-relative hyperaccess definitions of Turno 006 supersede the earlier wording.
