# Cover letter — Journal of Modern Dynamics

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“Nonnested extremal targets in a rational radix–Möbius semigroup”** by Facundo Firmenich.

The manuscript studies a noncommutative rational semigroup generated by an integer-radix transformation and a Möbius-conjugate branch. Its principal result is an exact arithmetic failure of nested optimality across three consecutive extremal targets: the states that first attain one target are not the best precursors of the next. The first four target times are certified as 45, 85, 120, and 162, and the nonnesting phenomenon is proved to persist on the exact positive-width parameter interval

\[
\kappa\in[10.6197,11.01).
\]

The paper does not claim novelty for radix transformations, Farey maps, or Bellman’s principle individually. Its contribution is the exact interaction between a rational radix–Möbius action, primitive arithmetic reduction, extremal hitting-time objectives, and the insufficiency of visible target height as a value variable.

The computational results use primitive integer arithmetic, exact target comparisons, exact state deduplication, independent continuation searches, and replayable ledgers. The paper and supplement explicitly state the evidence boundary. The unresolved depth-five optimum is not used in any theorem of the manuscript.

We believe the work fits the journal because it combines discrete and arithmetic dynamics, symbolic branch structure, exact hitting-time phenomena, and computer-assisted proof in a rational dynamical system.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. No public DOI is claimed before archival deposition. The submission package and public repository contain the code, compact exact certificates, replay scripts, ledgers, and hashes required to audit the manuscript claims:

https://github.com/FacundoFirmenich/rgphi-radix-mobius-dynamics

The active depth-five Stage B2 computation is explicitly outside the theorem dependency graph of this submission. Its 19.7 GB checkpoint payload is not part of the lightweight Paper I package, and no claim in the manuscript depends on completing that computation.

Sincerely,

Facundo Firmenich  
Barcelona, Spain
